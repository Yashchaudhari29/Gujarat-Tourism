# Editorial Correction Log

## Claims and Fact-Checking

### 1. Dholavira Size and Status
**Original Assumption/Claim:** Dholavira is "India's largest Harappan site."
**Correction/Editorial Action:** Changed to describe it as "one of the largest and most prominent archaeological sites in India." Rakhigarhi in Haryana is generally considered larger by area. The script emphasizes its unique water conservation system rather than disputable superlatives.
**Source/Date:** UNESCO World Heritage List (Ref: 1645), accessed 2026-09-11.

### 2. World's First Water Harvesting
**Original Assumption/Claim:** Dholavira contains the "world's first water-harvesting system."
**Correction/Editorial Action:** Changed to "among the earliest known advanced water conservation systems." This avoids absolute historical superlatives which can be disproven by future discoveries in other parts of the world.

### 3. Banni Grassland
**Original Assumption/Claim:** Banni is "Asia's largest grassland."
**Correction/Editorial Action:** Described as "one of the most expansive natural grasslands in the Indian subcontinent." We avoid unverified continental superlatives.

### 4. Lothal Dockyard
**Original Assumption/Claim:** Lothal was a "proven tidal dockyard."
**Correction/Editorial Action:** Described as a basin that is "interpreted" or "understood by many archaeologists" as a tidal dockyard. The script notes that the site is on the UNESCO Tentative List, not the fully inscribed World Heritage list.
**Source/Date:** UNESCO Tentative List (Ref: 5918), accessed 2026-09-11.

### 5. Vav-Tharad District Split
**Original Assumption/Claim:** Banaskantha includes Vav and Tharad.
**Correction/Editorial Action:** The scripts accurately reflect the administrative reorganization where Vav-Tharad was carved out of Banaskantha to form a new district. The scripts cover both the shared heritage and the distinct administrative identity.

## Principles Followed
- No invented dialogue or fictional historical characters.
- Extraneous motivational padding ("you must visit," "a heaven on earth") removed in favor of documentary-style observation.
- Current wildlife and industrial statistics are generalized to avoid rapid outdating, while citing official portals for exact figures.
