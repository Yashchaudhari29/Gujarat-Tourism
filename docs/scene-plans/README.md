# District Scene Plans

This document provides visual planning and pacing for the district narrations. Since the actual audio timings will depend on the narrator's pace, this serves as a structural guide.

## Scene Progression (Typical 10-15 Min Story)

1. **Title / Introduction (0:00 - 1:30)**
   - *Visual:* Wide landscape shot of the district's defining feature.
   - *Focus:* 50% 50%
   - *Content:* Establishing identity, borders, and general essence.

2. **Geography & Climate (1:30 - 3:30)**
   - *Visual:* Maps, rivers, agricultural fields, or terrain.
   - *Focus:* 30% 70% (panning effect)
   - *Content:* Topography, main rivers, climate impact on local life.

3. **History & Heritage (3:30 - 6:30)**
   - *Visual:* Key monuments, archaeological sites, or historical structures.
   - *Focus:* Specific architectural details.
   - *Content:* Foundational history, significant eras, and documented heritage.

4. **Economy & Crafts (6:30 - 9:00)**
   - *Visual:* Artisans at work, industrial zones, or traditional markets.
   - *Focus:* 40% 60%
   - *Content:* Traditional livelihoods, modern industries, and local arts.

5. **Ecology & Nature (9:00 - 11:30)**
   - *Visual:* Wildlife, forests, coastal areas, or wetlands.
   - *Focus:* 60% 40%
   - *Content:* Flora, fauna, conservation efforts.

6. **Culture & Conclusion (11:30 - End)**
   - *Visual:* Festivals, people, everyday life, transitioning to a sunset/closing shot.
   - *Focus:* 50% 50%
   - *Content:* Community life, future outlook, and closing thoughts.

## Topic Boundaries

When defining 	opics in story.json, map them roughly to these sections. For example:
- 	opic-history: 3:30 - 6:30
- 	opic-economy: 6:30 - 9:00
- 	opic-ecology: 9:00 - 11:30
